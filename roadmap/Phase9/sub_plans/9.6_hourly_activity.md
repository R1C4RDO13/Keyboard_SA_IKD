## Concept

Two widgets answering "*when in the day do I type?*":

1. **24-Hour Bar Chart** (range-scoped) — 24 bars, one per hour-of-day, height = keystrokes accumulated in that hour across the selected range.
2. **Hour × Weekday Heatmap** (always all-time) — 24 columns × 7 rows, cell intensity = total keystrokes for that hour-on-that-weekday across **all** the user's data.

Both live in the **Daily Activity** section (introduced in 9.5) — see [orchestrator Decision #17](../Phase9_Plan.md#cross-cutting-decisions). Sit beneath the calendar heatmap and daily keypress bar from 9.5.

The 24×7 heatmap is **always all-time** ([orchestrator Decision #18](../Phase9_Plan.md#cross-cutting-decisions)) — single-week data is too sparse to expose a circadian pattern; the chart card has an explicit "Across all your sessions" subtitle so the static range scope is documented in the UI.

## SQL

### Hourly (range-scoped)

```sql
SELECT
    CAST(strftime('%H', timestamp / 1000, 'unixepoch', 'localtime') AS INTEGER) AS hour,
    COUNT(*) AS keystrokeCount
FROM ikd_events
WHERE event_category != 'AUTOCORRECT'
  AND timestamp >= :fromMs
  AND timestamp <  :toMs
  AND (:moodScore IS NULL
       OR session_id IN (SELECT session_id FROM mood_entries WHERE mood_score = :moodScore))
GROUP BY hour
ORDER BY hour
```

Worst case: 24 rows.

### Hour × Weekday (all-time, range-independent)

```sql
SELECT
    CAST(strftime('%w', timestamp / 1000, 'unixepoch', 'localtime') AS INTEGER) AS dow,
    CAST(strftime('%H', timestamp / 1000, 'unixepoch', 'localtime') AS INTEGER) AS hour,
    COUNT(*) AS keystrokeCount
FROM ikd_events
WHERE event_category != 'AUTOCORRECT'
  AND (:moodScore IS NULL
       OR session_id IN (SELECT session_id FROM mood_entries WHERE mood_score = :moodScore))
GROUP BY dow, hour
ORDER BY dow, hour
```

Worst case: 168 rows. SQLite `%w` returns `0..6` with `0 = Sunday`; render `Mon` first per Western convention by reordering in the activity.

## UI spec

### 24-Hour Bar Chart
- 24 bars. X axis labels every 6 hours (`00`, `06`, `12`, `18`). Y = keystroke count.
- Range-scoped — when the user picks Week, only that week's keystrokes count.
- Mood-filter-respected (after 9.4 wires it through).
- Bar colour: `getProperPrimaryColor()`.

### Hour × Weekday Heatmap
- 24 columns × 7 rows. Cell colour = `getProperPrimaryColor()` at intensity = `count / max`.
- **Always all-time.** Range selector does not affect this card.
- Card subtitle below the title: "Across all your sessions" — visually documents the static scope.
- Tap a cell → toast `"<weekday> <hour:00>: <n> keystrokes"`.
- Empty cells: 5% alpha (same convention as the calendar heatmap).
