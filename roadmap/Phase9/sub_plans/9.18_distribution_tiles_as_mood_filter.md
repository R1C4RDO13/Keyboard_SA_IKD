# Phase 9.18 — Distribution tiles double as the Mood Filter

**Status:** Approved.
**Depends on:** Phase 9.4 (mood filter mechanic + `*ForMood` DAO queries), Phase 9.14.3 (Filters bottom sheet + active filter chip), Phase 9.17 (mood-color refresh + colored Distribution tiles).
**Branch:** land directly on `main`.
**Scope (one sentence):** Wire each of the six Distribution tiles on the Summary tab to act as a one-tap shortcut for the existing global **Mood Filter** — tap a tile to scope every chart and KPI on the dashboard to that mood; tap the active tile (or the active-filter chip ✕) to revert to All. The Distribution row itself keeps showing the **unfiltered** range distribution so it remains a useful filter-picker (Option A from the preview discussion).

---

## 1. Why

Today tapping a tile on the Summary tab shows a one-shot toast and nothing changes. The same Mood Filter mechanic already exists — but it lives buried inside the Filters bottom sheet (toolbar ⚙ → Filters → mood chip → Apply). Three taps to do what should be one. Tiles are visually prominent, color-coded, and tappable; they are the perfect surface for the most common mood-filter operation.

Hooking the existing `currentMoodFilter` state to tile clicks turns the Distribution row into a direct filter picker without taking real estate away from anything.

---

## 2. Behavior

- **Default state** (`currentMoodFilter == null`): all six tiles render normally, no highlight.
- **Tap an inactive tile** → `currentMoodFilter = score`, every aggregator re-runs through the Phase 9.4 `*ForMood` DAO paths, the active-filter chip below the TabLayout updates ("😊 Happy" or "Week · 😊 Happy"). The tapped tile gains an **active highlight**: a thin primary-coloured border (1.5 dp) plus a subtle 1.05× scale-up. Other tiles dim slightly (alpha 0.55).
- **Tap the active tile again** → `currentMoodFilter = null`, full dashboard reverts to All. Highlight removed.
- **Tap a *different* inactive tile while filtered** → switch the filter to that score (one tap, not two).
- **Tap the active-filter chip's `×`** → revert to All as today (chip already drives this); the tile highlight clears in sync.

### What the tiles themselves show during a filter

Per the preview discussion, **Option A**: tiles always render the **unfiltered** range distribution. The tile percentages do **not** re-aggregate when a filter is active. Reasoning:

- Re-aggregating would render the active tile at 100% and the other five at 0% — degenerate.
- Tiles double as the global color legend; they need to remain meaningful when filtered.
- Users can compare "this is my mood mix overall" against "this is my typing pattern when I'm angry" without losing context.

The other mood widget on the Summary tab — the Mood Mix stacked bar — **does** re-aggregate to the filtered scope (one tall segment of the active mood per bucket). That's already how Phase 9.4 wires it; no change needed there.

### Tap-toast (preserved)

The Phase 9.17 toast `"<emoji> <label> · <N> sessions (<P>%)"` keeps firing on tap so users still get the count read-out.

## 5. Decisions

| # | Topic | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tile percentages while filtered | **Option A — unfiltered.** Tiles remain a usable filter-picker and global legend regardless of active filter. |
| 2 | Active highlight | Primary-tinted stroke (1.5 dp) + 1.05× scale-up + 0.55 alpha on the other five. |
| 3 | Toggle semantics | Tap active tile → All. Tap different tile while filtered → switch (one tap, not "first clear then pick"). Mirrors Phase 8.2 mood-bar slot toggles. |
| 4 | Bottom sheet | Kept verbatim. Tile-tap and sheet-pick are equivalent operations on the same `currentMoodFilter` state. |
| 5 | Tap-toast | Preserved. Firing is independent of filter activation. |
| 6 | Commit shape | Single feat commit: `feat(phase9.18): tap mood tiles to scope dashboard by mood`. |
